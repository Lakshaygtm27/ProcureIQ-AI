STEP 4 — Build a deterministic scoring engine

This is the core of your application.

Suppose the buyer chooses:

Cost        = 40%
Quality     = 35%
Lead Time   = 25%

Normalize each metric.

For a benefit criterion:

Normalized Score =
Vendor Value / Maximum Value × 100

For a cost criterion:

Normalized Score =
Minimum Value / Vendor Value × 100

Then:

Final Score =
Cost Score × Cost Weight
+
Quality Score × Quality Weight
+
Lead-Time Score × Lead-Time Weight

Example:

Cost Score       = 92
Quality Score    = 95
Lead-Time Score  = 88

Weights:
Cost             = 40%
Quality          = 35%
Lead Time        = 25%

Final Score
= (92 × .40)
+ (95 × .35)
+ (88 × .25)

= 92.45

Then:

Rank vendors by Final Score DESC
